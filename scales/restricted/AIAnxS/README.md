# AIAnxS — Artificial Intelligence Anxiety Scale (bilingual)

**Restricted: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 — free for non-commercial research use, with attribution; no derivatives.**

21-item AI Anxiety Scale (Wang & Wang, 2019/2022), four factors: Learning (8),
Job Replacement (6), Sociotechnical Blindness (4), AI Configuration (3). Items rated
1–7; subscales averaged, total summed (21–147); higher = more AI anxiety.

## Languages
- **English (en)** — original items (Wang & Wang), the final 21-item set from the
  57-item pool; item IDs preserve the pool's Q-codes, presented in factor order.
- **German (de)** — validated translation (Hajek et al., 2025, *PLoS ONE*), mapped 1:1
  to the same items by factor order.
