Reprint permission statement
----------------------------
The English-language items of the Sport Motivation Scale are accompanied by the
following explicit reprint grant, which appears as the header of the bilingual
(English/Czech) item figure:

    "Reprinted from Pelletier et al., 1995, under a CC BY license, with
    permission from Luc Pelletier"

Where this came from
--------------------
The SMS was originally published in:

    Pelletier, L. G., Tuson, K. M., Fortier, M. S., Vallerand, R. J.,
    Brière, N. M., & Blais, M. R. (1995). Toward a new measure of intrinsic
    motivation, extrinsic motivation, and amotivation in sports: The Sport
    Motivation Scale (SMS). Journal of Sport and Exercise Psychology, 17(1),
    35-53. https://doi.org/10.1123/jsep.17.1.35

The verbatim English items, together with the reprint grant quoted above, were
reproduced in the open-access validation article:

    Komarc, M., Harbichová, I., & Scheier, L. M. (2020). Psychometric
    validation of Czech version of the Sport Motivation Scale. PLoS ONE,
    15(1), e0227277. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0227277

That article is published by PLoS ONE under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License (CC BY 4.0). Its supplementary item table presents the
original English SMS items alongside the Czech translation, and the figure header
records that the English items are reprinted from Pelletier et al. (1995) under a
CC BY license, with the original author Luc Pelletier's permission. The English
item wording in SMS.osd is taken from that table.

Note on item wording
---------------------
Items 21 (introjected regulation) and 22 (external regulation) in SMS.osd follow
the canonical Pelletier et al. (1995) wording. The English column of the Komarc
et al. (2020) figure contained two transcription errors at these positions
(item 21's English text duplicated item 22's; item 22 read "how good I am good
at"). The Czech translation in the same figure confirms the canonical wording
used here.
